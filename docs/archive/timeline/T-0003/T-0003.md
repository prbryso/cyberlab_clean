## Description

After defining the curriculum, I worked with Microsoft Copilot to develop the educational content for each module.

The emphasis was on creating technically accurate explanations that progressively introduced cybersecurity concepts while maintaining consistency across the curriculum.

The focus remained on educational quality rather than software implementation.
