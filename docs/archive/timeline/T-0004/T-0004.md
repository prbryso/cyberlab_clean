## The Decision to Build Cyber Lab

**Approximate Date**

## Description

As the volume of educational material continued to grow, it became clear that documents alone could not provide the learning experience I envisioned.

The decision was made to implement the curriculum as an interactive Flutter application.

Each module would become part of a structured learning environment capable of presenting information visually, guiding learners through progressively more advanced topics, and eventually supporting simulations and interactive demonstrations.

This application became known as **Cyber Lab**.

---

## Motivation

Transform static educational material into an interactive learning experience.

## Why It Mattered

This marked the transition from curriculum development to software engineering.

Cyber Lab became the vehicle through which the educational philosophy could be realized.

---

## Lessons Learned

Interactive software has the potential to teach complex concepts far more effectively than static documents.

The application became a teaching environment rather than simply a repository of information.
