## Description

The Systems Studio journey began when my grandson developed an interest in cybersecurity.

I wanted to help him understand the subject beyond what was available through introductory books and websites. Rather than simply recommending resources, I decided to create educational material that explained cybersecurity in a way that was technically accurate, engaging, and easy to understand.

At this stage there was no software project, no application architecture, and no long-term vision. The objective was simply to help one student learn.

---

## Motivation

Provide my grandson with a comprehensive introduction to cybersecurity that emphasized understanding rather than memorization.
