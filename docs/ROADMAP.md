---

# Guiding Principles

Every new feature should satisfy at least one of these:

✓ Makes the product easier to use

✓ Makes the product look more professional

✓ Improves learning

✓ Makes the product easier to sell

✓ Makes the product easier to administer

If it doesn't accomplish one of these, it waits.
