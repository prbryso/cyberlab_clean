## T-0006

### Discovering a General Educational Architecture

**Approximate Date**

(To be determined)

**Description**

While developing Cyber Lab, I realized that the underlying instructional approach was not limited to cybersecurity.

The same architecture could be applied to any discipline in which understanding systems was more valuable than memorizing isolated facts.

The project began to evolve from a cybersecurity application into a general educational framework.

This realization eventually led to the creation of Systems Studio.

**Motivation**

Generalize the educational architecture so it could support many technical and scientific subjects.

**People**

- Phillip Bryson

**Tools**

- ChatGPT

**Artifacts Produced**

- Early Systems Studio concepts
- Architectural notes
- Educational framework ideas

**Why It Mattered**

This was the conceptual birth of Systems Studio.

Cyber Lab became the first implementation of a much larger vision.

**Lessons Learned**

Strong architectures often emerge when solving one problem deeply enough that the underlying pattern becomes visible.
