## Description

Working with Microsoft Copilot, I began identifying the major knowledge areas required for a comprehensive cybersecurity curriculum.

Rather than immediately writing lessons, we first organized the subject into logical modules that would guide a learner from foundational concepts through increasingly advanced topics.

The objective was to ensure that the curriculum covered cybersecurity as a complete system rather than as isolated subjects.
